1. The 4d6 Guiding Bolt Nuke: You are a DPS monster! Fire Guiding Bolt to deal huge 4d6 Radiant damage. The next ally attacking the target gains Advantage!

3. Warding Flare Defense: When an enemy attacks you, use your Reaction to blind them with sunlight!
