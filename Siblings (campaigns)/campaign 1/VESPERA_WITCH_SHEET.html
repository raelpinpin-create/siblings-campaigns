📖 Witch's Grimoire Guide
1. Spells vs Cantrips: Cantrips (Poison Spray, Toll the Dead) can be cast infinitely! 1st-Level spells cost 1 spell slot.

2. Attack Roll vs Saving Throw: With Chill Touch and Ray of Sickness, YOU roll a d20+5 to hit their AC. With Poison Spray and Toll the Dead, the ENEMY rolls a save against your DC 13!

3. Lawful Evil Doctrine: You are calm, sophisticated, and ruthlessly efficient. You honor contracts to the letter and use poison to eliminate obstacles without messy violence.
